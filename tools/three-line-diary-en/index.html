3-Line Diary for People Who Don't Know What to Write

Don't know what to write in your diary?
Just answer three simple questions. Small things are enough.

1. What made you feel a little better today?
It can be something small, like a good meal or getting enough sleep.
2. What did you manage to do today?
Even “I got out of bed” counts.
3. What made you a little happy today?
It doesn't have to be a big event.
